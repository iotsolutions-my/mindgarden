# MindGarden Changelog

## v3.2.19 — Connected utilities, policy economics and high-rise redevelopment
- Removed false disconnected-plant warnings caused by city-wide percentage checks.
- Report active plant/substation counts, connected residents and production shortfalls separately.
- Add residential-density report with high-rise and apartment populations; allow in-place upgrades.
- Add differentiated income from Mayor policies based on visitors, employed residents, housing served by utilities and commuting.
- Cap city happiness and healthcare under extreme environmental pollution so serious hazards have real quality-of-life consequences.
- Preserve helicopter/aircraft rendering and complete city-save format.

## v3.2.18 — Planning transparency and milestone diagnostics
- Show traffic demand, transport capacity, current congestion, milestone target, and policy effects.
- Add scenario previews for roads, bus terminals, signals, subway and transport policy.
- Replace generic green-energy prompts with measurable power deficit, pollution and ESG summaries.
- Preserve existing helicopter and WebGL rendering code unchanged.

## v3.2.17 — Grid, mission and bridge repairs
- Restore green perimeter and grid tiles at all zoom levels.
- Replace high floating bridge slabs with supported near-road-level decks and visible access plates.
- Adjust vehicle bridge offset to match the reduced deck elevation.
- Clarify IoT sensor and signal placement for construction missions in all four languages.
- Repair interrupted mission state when save data has no active mission and there is no pending countdown; prevent missing mission-option translations from throwing.
- Preserve all earlier aircraft model geometry and rendering logic byte-for-byte.
- Retain maritime navigation, player-defined sea, Mayor's Office, and existing saved cities.

## v3.2.16 — Coastline and bridges
- New road bridges over sea tiles; connected sea routes remain navigable to ships.
- Ships can sail beyond a connected map-edge sea into the decorative background ocean.
- Coastlines are editable and extend into larger maps.

## v3.2.15 — Custom sea
- Sea and coastline added as editable map tiles; ports require connected open sea.

## v3.2.14 — Shipping and environment
- Shipping animation, container facilities, pollution indicators and port revenue.

## v3.2.13 — Green city and maritime economy
- ESG, alternative energy, seaports, cargo terminals, marina and Mayor planning refinements.

## v3.2.12 and earlier
- Mayor's Office, ten daily missions, transport, tourism and buildings, multilingual support, PWA, security baseline, and the original ten-game collection. Historical release details remain available in previous release packages.
