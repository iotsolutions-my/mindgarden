# MindGarden changelog

## v3.32.0 — Smart City: clouds, bigger pedestrians, close-ups in Watch mode
**Clouds**
- A few voxel clouds now drift slowly across the sky, with soft see-through shadows moving over the ground below.
- Bigger maps get more clouds (8 to 36), always scattered rather than covering the whole sky.
- On rainy days extra grey clouds roll in and the wind picks up. At night the cloud shadows fade.
- Clouds fade out as you zoom in, and are gone completely at close zoom, so they never hide what you are building.
- Clouds stop drifting while the game is paused, and they appear in postcards.

**Pedestrians**
- People are about 70% bigger, so they can be seen walking at normal zoom.
- More clothing colours.

**Watch mode**
- Every third stop is a close-up on someone walking along the street.

## v3.30.4 — Seed Rush: more responsive phone controls
- Swipe anywhere on the screen to turn, even below the maze, so your thumb does not cover the ladybird. Turns happen while your finger is still moving.
- Corner assist: a slightly late swipe still turns at the corner you just passed, and an early swipe waits for the next corner.
- Tapping a spot still sends the ladybird there. It now keeps running afterwards instead of stopping.
- On touch screens the robots are 12% slower, and they must come a little closer before they catch you.
- Seed Rush build 3.20.3.

## v3.30.3 — Seed Rush: tap or hold to move on phones
- Seed Rush is now easy to steer on a phone. Tap any spot on the maze and the ladybird walks there by itself along the shortest route, then stops. A pulsing red ring shows where it is heading.
- Keep your finger on the maze and the ladybird follows it as you move.
- Tapping a hedge picks the nearest path the ladybird can reach.
- The arrow keys and arrow buttons still work as before.
- Seed Rush build 3.20.2.

## v3.30.2 — Smart City: air-show smoke instead of the banner
- The tourism helicopter now trails 5 ribbons of coloured smoke (red, orange, yellow, green and blue), like an air show, instead of a plain banner.
- The smoke widens and fades to white as it drifts behind the helicopter.
- The "✈️ Visit Malaysia!" or "🧳 Cuti-cuti Malaysia!" sign now follows the helicopter itself.

## v3.30.1 — Smart City fix: aircraft clear tall buildings; no drawn flags
- Fixed: drones and helicopters could fly through high-rise buildings. They used to fly at a fixed height.
  - Each flight path now measures the real height of every building under and beside it, then flies above the tallest one.
  - This covers airport helicopters, drones and the Visit Malaysia banner helicopter.
  - Flight heights update automatically as the city grows.
- Removed all drawn flags, because a simplified flag can look like an incorrect national flag:
  - The banner is now plain white with a gold edge. The sign above it reads "✈️ Visit Malaysia!" or "🧳 Cuti-cuti Malaysia!".
  - Street decorations for Merdeka and Malaysia Day are now gold and blue festive pennants.
  - Flag emoji are replaced: 🎆 Merdeka Day, 🎊 Malaysia Day and 🎉 on the celebration card.

**New missions**

## v3.29.0 — Smart City: Malaysian festivals at national scale, local food and heritage
**National celebrations now involve most of the city**
- Major festivals are celebrated by most Malaysians at open houses, in their neighbourhoods and at places of worship, so they now draw most of the city instead of a few hundred people:
  - Merdeka Day: about 90% of residents.
  - Malaysia Day, Hari Raya Aidilfitri and Chinese New Year: about 85%.
  - Deepavali and the Malaysian open house: about 80%.
  - Gawai and Kaamatan, and the Mid-Autumn lantern festival: about 70%.
  - Wesak Day: about 65%.
  - Thaipusam: about 60% of residents, but the highest share of visitors from other towns.
- Residents celebrate at home and in their own neighbourhood, so venues no longer cap how many residents take part. Parks, roads, food places, places of worship and heritage sites decide how many visitors the city can welcome.
- Rewards grow with the crowd, so national festivals are now a good source of income for small towns.
- Concerts, expos and other ticketed events keep the venue-based model. Gotong-royong stays at about 30%.
- The result line is in all 4 languages, for example: "(340 participants · 85% of residents celebrating · 30 visitors)".
- The mission card shows "National celebration" with residents celebrating, the share of residents, visitors expected and room for visitors.

**New celebrations**
- 🇲🇾 Malaysia Day (16 September), 🌾 Gawai and Kaamatan harvest festival, 🪷 Wesak Day and 🏮 Mid-Autumn lantern festival.
- 🍛 Malaysian food festival (nasi lemak, roti canai, char kuey teow, satay, teh tarik). It earns more for each food place in the city.
- 🏛️ Heritage walk. It earns more for each heritage building.
- Merdeka, CNY, Hari Raya, Deepavali and Thaipusam have new descriptions and options that reflect open-house culture. The stewarded option can close a street for the day for every major festival.

**Festive decorations**
- After a celebration is held, the city is decorated for the rest of that day:
  - Merdeka and Malaysia Day: Jalur Gemilang flags.
  - Chinese New Year: red lanterns.
  - Hari Raya and the open house: pelita lamps.
  - Deepavali: kolam patterns and oil lamps.
  - Mid-Autumn: coloured lanterns.
  - Wesak: yellow lanterns.
- Lanterns and lamps glow at night.

**New local food and heritage buildings** (in "🍜 Local food & heritage")

| ☕ Kopitiam (heritage coffee shop) | RM 650 | start | +6 | +RM 110/day |

- Kopitiam: marble tables and a red signboard.
- Mamak: zinc awning, plastic stools and a "24 hours" sign that lights up at night.
- Kampung house: stilts, a coconut tree and wooden stairs.
- Heritage shophouses: a row of 3 pastel shophouses with a five-foot way and red lanterns.

- Cultural performance centre: a curved-horn roof and an open-air stage with dancers.

- The food court is now called "Food court / hawker centre", because the mamak has its own building.
- New building goals for the kopitiam, mamak, kampung house, shophouses and museum.

## v3.28.1 — Smart City fix: clean mission list each day
- Fixed: a new day could show ✓ ticks on missions not yet played. Cause: pressing Undo after Next Day took the city back to the previous day while keeping the new day's mission records, so the same day number came round twice.
- Undo now works only within the current day ("Nothing to undo" after Next Day).
- Ticks show only for missions already handled today, and saves already affected repair themselves when loaded.
- The "Today:" line now matches the real number of missions (for example "8 missions · 3 city incidents · 2 building goals · 3 community events") instead of the old fixed "4 missions".

## v3.28.0 — Smart City: quicker building and a plot card instead of Inspect
**Quick tools**
- The quick row now has 8 large buttons with icons and prices: Road, Bulldozer, Sea (free), River, Water pond / reservoir, Park, Wetland reserve and Bridge. No more scrolling through the building list for water and parks.
- Labels follow the chosen language, and buttons not yet unlocked show the level they need.

**Tap a building to see what it is (Inspect removed)**
- Tapping any existing building, road or water tile, with any tool selected, opens a card on the map showing what it is, its location, road connection and what it does.
- The card offers:
  - ⬆️ Upgrade buttons with the price (House → Apartment block or Condominium, Apartment → Condominium, Shop → Shopping mall, Factory → Clean factory). Locked upgrades show the level they need.
  - 🗺️ Show coverage for police, fire, health, transport and community buildings.
  - 🚜 Remove with the refund amount (larger refunds ask to confirm).
- Your selected tool stays selected, so you can keep building straight after. Tapping an empty plot builds as before.
- Holding an upgrade tool (for example Shopping mall on a shop) still upgrades in one tap.
- The Inspect button is gone; resident speech bubbles hide while the card is open.

**Phone layout**
- The cash card on the map is narrower on phones so it no longer covers the map buttons.

## v3.27.0 — Smart City: smoother traffic and growing shops
**Traffic**
- Fixed: some cars and emergency vehicles looked like they were spinning on the spot. They had very short routes (1–2 road tiles) and turned around every half-second.
- Every vehicle now gets a route of at least 3 road tiles, pauses briefly at each end before turning back, and turns around at most once every 3 seconds.

**Shops grow with the city**
- Upgrade a shop yourself: choose Shopping mall and tap a shop. You pay only the difference (RM 1,850), just like upgrading houses to apartments.
- Shop owners expand on their own: from Regional city size, when residents want more shops, one busy shop with plenty of homes nearby becomes a mall each day (turn off with the same "Residents may upgrade" switch in City administration).
- Shophouses get taller as the city grows: 2 storeys in a village, 3 in a town, and 4 with a lit rooftop sign in a city.
- 🔎 Inspect now tells you how to upgrade houses, apartments, shops and factories, and how much it costs.

## v3.26.0 — Smart City: missions grow with your city
Missions are the main source of income for a small city, so they now follow the city's size:

| City level | Missions per day | Penalties | Rewards | New missions |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 🌱 Village (under 120 residents) | 8 | **None** | normal | — |
| 🏘️ Growing town (120+) | 8 | Small (half) | normal | 🧹 Gotong-royong day |
| 🏙️ Regional city (360+) | 10 | Full | +20% | 🎤 Big concert night, 🚨 Street protest (police can be dispatched) |
| 🌆 Metropolitan city (900+) | 12 | Heavier (×1.5) | +40% | 🌏 World Expo (big reward with a World Trade Centre or event hall) |

- In a village, deferring a mission or leaving missions unfinished costs nothing, so a small city can't run out of money from penalties.
- Unfinished missions at Next Day are capped at 4 for penalty purposes.
- Bigger cities get one to three tough missions a day. Concerts use the stadium or sports centre; the World Expo pays more when you have visitors, hotels and an airport.
- The Missions tab now explains the current rules under your city level.
- A day already in progress keeps its mission count; the new count starts from the next day.
- Fixed: the "All buildings are available" note ran into the level text, and the finished-day summary said "The next one is coming" when all missions were done. Deferred incidents no longer say "Skip this building mission".

## v3.25.0 — Smart City Phase 4: airports, bridges, coverage and money you can see
**Airports with real runways**
- A new airport now comes with a terminal, control tower and a 4-tile runway, laid out automatically in a straight line beside the terminal (it needs 4 empty tiles in a row).
- Planes land on the runway, roll to the terminal, turn around, take off and climb away, instead of flying into the building. A small plane is parked at the gate. Runway edge lights glow at night.
- Existing airports get a runway automatically when there is room; otherwise they keep the old flight path.
- Bulldozing any part of the runway removes the airport (with the usual partial refund).

**Bridges and rivers that bend**
- Bridges are drawn like roads: straight sections, corners, T-junctions and crossings, with railings only on the open sides.
- Rivers join their neighbouring river, pond, sea and wetland tiles, so curves and bends look continuous, with sandy banks.
- Roads can now cross rivers: building a road on a river tile makes a road bridge (RM 480). Bulldozing it gives the river back.

**Smoke and steam**
- Fixed: some nuclear plants and factories had no smoke when the city had many of them. Smoke now follows what's on screen, and both nuclear cooling towers release steam.

**Coverage maps for more services**
- Coverage now shows for the Police station, Fire station, Hospital, Clinic, Bus terminal, Subway station, Taxi stop, and community buildings (Community hall, Mosque/Surau, Temple, Church, Event hall).
- It also shows when you use 🔎 Inspect on one of these buildings.
- A legend on the map explains the colours: quick to reach, slow to reach, too far.

**Money always visible**
- A 💰 cash card on the map shows your cash, the expected daily balance (▲ profit or ▼ loss per day) and the price of the building you've selected. It turns orange when cash is low or the city is losing money.
- The daily balance uses exactly the same calculation as Next Day.
- Every build, bulldoze and refund shows a short "−RM" or "+RM" label above the plot.
- Cash is now the first number in the stats row.

All 7 Smart City regression tests and the earlier browser checks still pass.

## v3.24.0 — Smart City Phase 3: reasons to come back
**Simpler screen, map first**
- The map now comes first on phones, with Undo and Next Day right below it.
- Everything else sits in four tabs: 🛠️ Build · 🎯 Missions · 📊 Reports · 🏛️ Mayor. A dot on Missions shows when something needs you.
- On wide screens the map stays on the left while you use the tabs on the right. The stats row scrolls sideways on phones, and the compass moved to the bottom corner.

**City levels and landmarks**
- Four levels by population: Village → Growing town (120) → Regional city (360) → Metropolitan city (900). Each level unlocks new buildings, with a celebration that lists what's new.
- The Missions tab shows your level, a progress bar and the next unlocks. Locked buildings appear greyed out with the level they need.
- Three new one-of-a-kind landmarks, each with its own 3D model, extra visitors, happiness and daily income: 🕰️ Clock Tower (Growing town), 🗼 Sky Tower (Regional city) and 🌆 Twin Towers (Metropolitan city).
- **Existing cities keep every building unlocked.** Levels lock buildings only in new cities (after "Reset city"). Daily missions never ask for a locked building.

**Daily Mayor's report card**
- Each Next Day gives a grade from A to F based on happiness, safety, health, water, power, traffic and missions handled, with an encouraging note, your best area and the area that needs work.
- A "Help me fix it" button picks a building that helps. A good-mayor streak counts days in a row graded A or B.
- The Reports tab keeps your last 7 report cards; tap one to see it again.

**Scenario maps** (Missions tab)
- 🏝️ Island Town (20 days): grow to 480 residents, open a seaport, keep happiness at 65%.
- 🌊 River City (25 days, floods are common): 650 residents, safety and health at 70%, 2 wetland reserves.
- ⛈️ After the Storm (15 days): restore water and power to 80% and happiness to 65%.
- Earn 1–3 stars depending on how quickly you finish. Scenarios have their own saves, and "Return to my city" takes you back to your own city exactly as you left it.

**Postcards and city name**
- 📷 on the map saves a framed postcard with your city's name, day, population, level and latest grade, or opens the share sheet on phones.
- Name your city in the Mayor tab.

All 7 Smart City regression tests and the Phase 2 browser checks still pass.

## v3.23.0 — Smart City Phase 2: gameplay that matters
**Emergencies on the map**
- Fires, crime, vandalism, road accidents and dengue calls now appear on the map: flames and smoke for fires, flashing beacons for police calls, a red marker for medical calls.
- Tap the flashing spot (or the first mission button) to send a real crew: a 🚒 fire truck, 🚓 police car or 🚑 ambulance leaves the nearest connected station and drives there along your roads.
- Response time depends on where you built your stations. Arriving within 10 seconds earns +RM 150, and within 20 seconds +RM 70. Slow responses suggest building closer.
- The mission button shows the expected arrival time. With no station connected by road, it says so, and you can still hire outside help.
- Coverage view: choose the Police, Fire station, Hospital or Clinic tool to see which roads are reached quickly (green), slowly (yellow), and which homes are too far (red).

**A friendlier daily rhythm**
- 4 missions a day instead of 10 (2 incidents, 1 building goal, 1 community event), with a short 4-second pause instead of 15 seconds.
- **Next Day is never locked.** Unfinished missions add a little disruption and reset the streak, but the game never stops you.
- Older saves keep the first four missions of the current day.

**Residents have a voice**
- Speech bubbles above homes say what residents need: a clinic, a park, shops, a bus stop, police, a fire station, a road, water, power, jobs, or less traffic. Happy neighbourhoods say so too.
- Tap a bubble to pick the building that helps. The 💬 button turns bubbles on or off.

**Demand bars and a growing city**
- A "What residents want" bar shows demand for Homes, Shops and Jobs. Tap a bar to choose a matching building.
- Residents now upgrade their own homes for free: in happy, connected neighbourhoods with water, power, shops and jobs, houses become apartments and apartments become condominiums, behind the construction crane. Turn this on or off under City administration.

**Smaller changes**
- New, smaller traffic lights with red, amber and green lamps, and a slimmer IoT sensor.
- All 7 existing Smart City regression tests still pass. Your city save is unchanged.

## v3.20.0 — Category menu, new Sentence Detective, Logic Squares fix
**Main menu**
- The home screen now shows four large category buttons: Words & Language, Memory & Attention, Numbers & Logic, and Big games. Tapping one opens that category's own game list. No sideways scrolling and no long page to scroll through.
- **Back** returns to the screen you came from (game → category → home). If a game was opened from a bookmark, Back goes to its category. A 🏠 button jumps straight home from anywhere.

**Sentence Detective (English and Malay), new content**
- Replaced the old 10 paragraphs (reused five times each) with **100 different passages per language**: 25 for each comfort level, from simple daily-life sentences (Relax) to linking words and cause and effect (Challenge) and precise vocabulary (Expert).

**Logic Squares fix**
- Fixed: a correctly filled grid was sometimes not accepted, because some puzzles had more than one valid answer and only the stored one counted. Puzzles are now generated with exactly one solution, and any complete grid with no clashes counts as a win.
